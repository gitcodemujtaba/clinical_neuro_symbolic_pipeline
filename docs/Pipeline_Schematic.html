Pipeline schematic — end to end

MIMIC-IV discharge note → SNOMED/OMOP concept, or human review

Five stages, one note at a time: abbreviation expansion, zero-shot entity extraction, tiered concept normalization, a three-model LLM ensemble tier gate, then routing to an autonomous knowledge-graph write or a human reviewer. Every label below is a real module, function, or threshold in the live codebase — nothing here is illustrative.
real run, 5 fresh notes · 373 entities
AUTO coverage · 56.6%
AUTO-tier precision · 92.1%
ensemble · qwen2.5:3b + llama3.2:3b + phi4-mini

01 The full route

Top to bottom: Stage 1 preprocessing, Stage 2a extraction, Stage 2b grounding, Stage 3's tier gate, Stage 4 routing. Diamonds are real decision points; the seven boxed outcomes at the bottom are the only places an entity's journey actually ends.
